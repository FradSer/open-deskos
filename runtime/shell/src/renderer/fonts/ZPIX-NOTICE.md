## Rendering

Upstream describes a 12px design grid (11px glyph plus 1px spacing), with 21998 English, simplified Chinese, traditional Chinese, and Japanese characters. The Pixel theme uses the Regular face for all of those scripts and disables synthesized weight/style. Other themes keep their existing fonts.
